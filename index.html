The Process

Up and Running in 3 Steps

No ML expertise needed. Users can plug-in their knowledge and get a live persona.

1

Upload Your Knowledge Base

Users can upload text documents or PDFs. Our pipeline ingests and indexes everything using Retrieval Augmented Generation.

2

Customize Your Persona

Give your AI persona a name, personality, and tone of voice. It becomes the face of your brand — knowledgeable, consistent, and always available.

3

Deploy & Start Chatting

Share your persona with others or keep it private. Start conversations instantly and get accurate, real-time answers drawn from your knowledge base.

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. Instead of relying purely on a pre-trained AI model's memory, RAG first searches through your uploaded documents to find relevant information, then uses that as context when generating a response. This means answers are accurate, up-to-date, and grounded in your actual content — not guesswork.
